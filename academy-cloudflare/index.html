Before. Build. After.

Teaching will combine concise concept videos, executable examples, troubleshooting and evidence-led assignments. A full instructional video library is not yet published.

BEFORE

Define the problem

Identify inputs, expected output, risk, manual effort and measurable success criteria.

BUILD

Implement and test

Connect the nodes, make decisions explicit and reproduce failures before fixing them.

AFTER

Demonstrate evidence

Verify the result, document limitations and explain how a real client might use it.
